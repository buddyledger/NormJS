Scaled Normal Distribution

The red curve is centred on the null value X₀: it shows where sample means would fall if H₀ were true, and its shaded tails are the rejection region. The blue curve is centred on the observed mean m: its shaded middle is the confidence interval. They have the same width because they share one SE, which is why the test and the interval always agree.
